Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle information collected for puja, ritual, and temple service bookings.

We value the trust of every devotee and visitor. This page explains what information may be collected through our website for puja, ritual, and temple service bookings, and how it may be used and protected.

1. Information We Collect

At Shri Baglamukhi Mata Mandir, we collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Book puja, ritual, consultation, or temple services
  • Submit a booking request or contact form
  • Request updates or communication related to temple services
  • Contact us through our website, phone, email, or other channels

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used to:

  • Process puja and temple service bookings and issue booking receipts
  • Communicate about booking status, schedule, puja details, and temple services
  • Respond to your inquiries and requests
  • Improve our website, booking process, and temple service experience
  • Send service-related updates only where applicable and with your consent

3. Information Protection

We implement appropriate administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies to enhance user experience and improve functionality. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect some website features.

5. Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties without your consent, except when required by law or when working with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website and temple services.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe such information has been collected, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage visitors to review this policy periodically.
