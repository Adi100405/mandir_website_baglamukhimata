Our Sacred Temple

Shri Shakti Mandir & Shri Shayam Kripa Dham

Shri Shakti Mandir is a beacon of divine power and devotion, serving the community for over seven decades. Our temple is blessed by Maa Baglamukhi — the eighth Mahavidya, goddess of power — and Shri Khatu Shyamji, together providing divine protection and blessings to all devotees.

We blend ancient Vedic traditions with modern convenience — offering puja, hawan and ritual service bookings with trusted temple guidance.

Shri Shakti Mandir is a revered spiritual centre and a beacon of divine power, faith, and devotion, serving the community with dedication for more than seven decades. For generations, devotees have visited the temple to seek divine blessings, spiritual strength, inner peace, and a deeper connection with the divine.

The temple is blessed by the presence and worship of Goddess Baglamukhi, the eight Mahavidya and a powerful manifestation of divine energy, traditionally revered for protection, strength, and the ability to overcome obstacles. The temple also worships Baba Khatu Shyam, who is deeply revered by devotees for his compassion, blessings, and unwavering support. Together, these sacred traditions make Shri Shakti Mandir a place where devotees can seek divine grace, protection, strength, and spiritual guidance.

Deeply rooted in ancient Vedic traditions and time-honoured rituals, Shri Shakti Mandir also embraces modern convenience to make devotional services more accessible to devotees. The temple offers convenient booking for puja, hawan, and other sacred rituals, along with trusted temple guidance to ensure that every ceremony is conducted with authenticity, devotion, and respect for tradition.

For over seventy years, Shri Shakti Mandir has remained committed to preserving its spiritual heritage while continuing to serve the evolving needs of its community. It is a sacred space where timeless traditions, sincere devotion, and modern accessibility come together welcoming every devotee to seek blessings, peace, protection, and divine grace.

Our Presiding Deity

Shri Khatu
Shyamji

Shri Khatu Shyamji is revered as the "Haare Ka Sahara" (support of the defeated). Devotees believe that whoever comes to His feet with a pure heart never leaves empty-handed.

The divine presence of Shyamji at our mandir blesses devotees with courage, prosperity, and protection. Special bhog and aarti are offered every Monday and on Ekadashi.

Baba Khatu Shyam is lovingly revered as "Haare Ka Sahara," the divine support of those who have lost hope or are facing difficult times. Devotees place their faith in His compassion and believe that anyone who approaches Him with a sincere and pure heart receives His blessings and divine grace.

The sacred presence of Baba Khatu Shyam Ji at our mandir offers devotees a place to seek strength, courage, prosperity, peace, and protection. His blessings are deeply cherished by those who turn to Him with faith and devotion, especially during challenging moments in life.

To honour Baba Khatu Shyam, special bhog and aarti are offered every Monday and on Ekadashi, providing devotees with an opportunity to participate in worship, express their devotion, and seek His divine blessings.
